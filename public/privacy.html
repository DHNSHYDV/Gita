Privacy Policy

Shreemad Bhagavad Gita App • Effective Date: September 2026

Our Sacred Commitment: The Shreemad Bhagavad Gita app is developed with deep reverence for spiritual seekers. We prioritize user privacy and adhere strictly to Google Play Developer Policies. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not display commercial advertising.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary information required to deliver an inspiring and seamless devotional experience:

  • Google Account Information (Optional): When you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and avatar image via Google Identity Services. This enables cloud backup and synchronization across devices.
  • Devotional Progress & Preferences: Your spiritual reading streaks, sadhana points, listening records, bookmarked shlokas, preferred reading language (Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, English), theme (light/dark), and text size are saved to provide continuous spiritual progress.
  • Local Notifications Data: If you grant notification permission, scheduled morning wisdom shlokas (at 6:30 AM) are scheduled locally on your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

Collected data is used solely to:

  • Personalize your experience with your preferred sacred name and language.
  • Synchronize bookmarks, streaks, and audio milestones between your devices.
  • Deliver daily morning Gita verses at 6:30 AM (if enabled in settings).
  • Maintain the optional community sadhana leaderboard.

3. Third-Party Services

We partner only with reputable cloud infrastructure providers to run the application:

  • Google Identity Services: Provides secure OAuth authentication for users who opt to sign in with Google. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Supabase: Encrypted PostgreSQL cloud database that securely stores devotee profiles, bookmarks, and sadhana points over HTTPS/SSL. (Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • Capgo: Delivers over-the-air web asset updates strictly compliant with Google Play Distribution Agreement Section 4.5.

4. Account & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

You have full ownership of your data and can request deletion at any time without restriction:

🗑️ How to Delete Your Account and Data

Method 1 (Instant in-app deletion):

Open the app ➔ Go to More tab ➔ Tap Delete Account & Data ➔ Confirm deletion. Your profile, bookmarks, reading streaks, sadhana points, and Google account linkage are permanently and immediately erased from our Supabase servers and local device storage.

Method 2 (Web / Email deletion request):

If you have uninstalled the app or prefer to request data deletion remotely, please email developer Dhanush Yadav at dhanushyadavkrish@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Gita Account". All associated cloud records will be permanently purged within 7 business days.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately so we can remove the data.

6. Security of Your Data

We implement industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted between the Gita app and our backend servers. Your credentials are never stored in plain text.

7. Policy Updates

We may periodically update this policy to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated effective date.
